LOCATION
- Garden City, Cairo: a prestigious, historically rich neighbourhood with a European design, tree-lined streets, curving roads, and a mix of Art Deco architecture and modern developments.
- Established in the early 20th century as a residential and diplomatic district. Home to several embassies, including the U.S. and British embassies.
- Bordered by the Nile to the west and Qasr al-Aini Street to the east; close to Tahrir Square.
- "In the vibrant heart of Downtown Cairo, a tranquil experience awaits."
- Key features of the area: residential and diplomatic use, architecture, green spaces, cultural significance.
- Nearby amenities: landmarks, recreation, restaurants and hotels.
- Urban context: well connected to Cairo's downtown while keeping a tranquil atmosphere. 150 m from the Garden City Nile.
